Build a multi-page streetwear ecommerce website for "Worship The Fumes"
(WTF), a GenZ direct-to-consumer clothing brand. Site-wide motto and voice:
"Weird is a choice." Voice is bold, confident, anti-mainstream, playful —
never corporate. Design should feel edgy, high-impact and unmistakable.

## Pages (4)
1. Home — Hero, Latest Drop, The WTF Way, Flagship Spotlight,
   The Cult Speaks, Join the Cult (in that order).
2. Shop — page header, collection gallery, CTA.
3. About — page header, origin story, values, CTA.
4. Contact — page header, contact form, CTA.
Shared navbar + footer on all pages.

## Brand palette (exact hexes)
- Neutral (near-black): #0A0A0A
- Primary "Acid" (lime): #C6FF00
- Secondary "Toxic" (magenta/pink): #FF2E88
- Tertiary "Violet": #7C3AED

## Typography
- Headings: Anton, Extra Large weight Bold, Tight line height,
  Loose letter spacing, UPPERCASE, narrow text width.
- Body: Space Grotesk, Large size, Regular line height, regular width.
- These two are the only brand fonts.

## Shape & spacing
- Radius: Sharp (0). Stroke: Medium. Containers carry borders.
- Spacing: Spacious vertical + horizontal, Spacious card padding,
  Regular container width.

## Element styling
- Primary buttons: fill = Acid (#C6FF00), Flat style, Chunky padding,
  Regular radius, UPPERCASE, Bold, Loose tracking, body font.
- Secondary buttons: bordered, subtle, match primary.
- Link color: neutral.
- Inputs: match primary, Bordered, subtle, Regular radius.
- Taglines: from-scheme accent, Fill treatment, UPPERCASE, semibold.
- Icons: Outlined, weight 500, from-scheme accent, Fill treatment.

## Design schemes (4 dark/bold colourways)
1. Scheme 1 — near-black base: bg #0A0A0A, secondary surface #111111,
   heading/body #F5F5F5, accent lime, white-alpha border.
2. Scheme 2 — clean white/light: bg white, secondary #F5F5F5,
   heading/body #0A0A0A, accent olive lime, dark-alpha border.
3. Scheme 3 — acid lime band: bg #88AF00, secondary #6F8E00,
   heading/body #0A0A0A, accent near-black.
4. Scheme 4 — toxic pink band: bg #DF2877, secondary #FF2E88,
   heading/body white, accent light lime.
Scheme rhythm on Home: dark (hero) → dark (drop) → light (WTF Way) →
acid (flagship) → dark (cult) → pink (CTA/join).
